# 01. Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
**EduGenie: Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant**

## Problem Identified
Students may find complex educational concepts difficult to understand and may need quick support for questions, summaries, quizzes, and structured learning guidance.

## Project Idea
EduGenie is a lightweight AI-powered educational assistant that uses generative AI to simplify learning.

## Main Ideas / Features
- Ask questions and receive smart, concise answers.
- Explain complex concepts in simplified language.
- Generate quizzes from topics or text.
- Summarize large educational passages.
- Provide personalized learning recommendations.

## Target Users
EduGenie is designed for students and self-learners across different academic levels.

## Example Use Scenarios
1. Asking a question such as “Which is the largest ocean?”
2. Generating a quiz to check understanding of a topic such as the Pythagoras Theorem.
3. Requesting a structured learning path for a topic such as SQL.

## Proposed Solution
A simple web-based learning assistant with a FastAPI backend and HTML/CSS frontend, supported by AI models for educational tasks.
